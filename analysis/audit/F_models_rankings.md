# F. Models, authors, families and rankings: data audit

Data audited: local copy of `/opt/modelpulse/data` (build of 2026-10-04 14:20 UTC, last day 2026-10-04, 683 snapshot days), live API for cross-checks.
Scripts: `F_common.py`, `F_01..F_15*.py` next to this file (they read the copy via `MP_DATA`; F_01-F_14 build a 47k-model working subset: top 40k by dl30, top 20k by dl_all, top 5k by dl_7d).
Limits: no server job was run (a local memory incident stopped heavy local runs; F_14b was killed and not rerun). Historical recomputations use today's `skip_days`, and the subset only contains models that are big today, so artifact counts are, if anything, understated.

## Headline findings

### 1. Author and family charts have a fake spike on 2025-02-27 (all-time series is 0, not null, before then). Severity HIGH, a reviewer will see it at once
- What: `author_series.dl_all` and `family_series.dl_all` are `0` on every day before 2025-02-27 (polars `sum` of nulls is 0). Per-model series correctly have null there. The web `daily()` treats the 0 as a real value, so the first all-time value becomes one day of downloads.
- Numbers: google-bert 0 -> 2,713,819,507 on 2025-02-27 (63% of its all-time total, 1,271x its median daily increment); sentence-transformers +2.42B; MIT +2.17B (99% of its total); FacebookAI +1.75B; openai +1.72B; microsoft +1.52B. Family: Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B family 0 -> 7,213,220. Verified on the live API (`/api/author/google-bert`: dl_all 0 on 02-26, 2,713,819,507 on 02-27).
- Where: AuthorPage opens on "Weekly" with no range selector, so the first bar (week of 2025-02-24) dwarfs everything after it; the "Daily" view and the family toggle in ModelPage do the same; the "All time" chart is a cliff from 0.
- Scope: 784 of 50,614 authors have a step > 25x their median daily step and > 1M; the top ones are all this single day.
- Fix: write null instead of 0 in author/family series when no member has `dl_all` (`sum` then `when(count>0)`), or have the API drop rows where dl_all=0 and the day is < 2025-02-27. `daily()` could also ignore a first step > N x the following median.

### 2. 2025-11-24 is a partial snapshot (26% of the usual rows), in neither `skip_days` nor `low_days`. Severity HIGH for author/family pages, a reviewer will likely raise it
- What: series has 289,425 rows on 2025-11-24 against about 1.13M on neighbours (1,130,658 on 11-23, 1,134,263 on 11-25); the only day below 96% of the local median in the whole history (script F_11). The hub series is unaffected (diffs per model), model pages are unaffected (rows simply missing, gap spread), but author and family sums collapse for one day.
- Numbers: amazon dl_all 497.7M (11-23) -> 11.4M (11-24) -> 498.0M (11-25), members 21 -> 11 -> 21, dl30 7.08M -> 3.96M -> 7.22M; the Weekly chart gets a fake +486.6M bar. unsloth 144.3M -> 39.6M -> 145.1M (1044 -> 155 -> 1059 members), MaziyarPanahi +306.7M, mradermacher +126.2M (51,906 -> 292 members), OpenMed +119.4M. 6,066 of 19,840 authors lost over 20% of members that day, 6,790 had a dl30 dip over 10%. Families: Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B family members 5475 -> 15 -> 5482, fake step +51.4M; Llama-3.1-8B family +49.6M.
- Contradiction: Wrapped (per-model increments) is correct for this day, the author page is not (amazon author page weekly bar 486M vs Wrapped best week 14.2M).
- Fix: add 2025-11-24 to `skip_days` (the API then drops it from model/family/author series), and add a pipeline guard: a snapshot with row count < ~90% of the median of its neighbours is a skip day. A row-count check in daily.py would catch the next one.

### 3. AuthorPage headline tiles sum only the 200 models the API returns. Severity HIGH, easy to spot
- What: `author_models(limit=200)` feeds `tot` in AuthorPage (all time, 30 days, 7 days, likes), labelled "downloads all time, across all models". The chart on the same page and the leaderboard use the full author_series.
- Numbers: mradermacher (70,529 models) tile shows 20.7M all time and 5.57M in 30 days; chart end and leaderboard say 316,993,041 and 44,272,199 (tile = 6.5% / 12.6%). bartowski tile 101.99M vs 154.28M all time (66%); unsloth 387.8M vs 522.97M; OpenMed 61%. 663 authors have more than 200 models; 397 of them show less than 90% of their 30-day total, 98 less than 50%; 9 of 37 authors above 1M monthly downloads.
- Fix: use `store.author_summary()` (already exists, full table) or the last point of the series for the tiles; the "200+ tracked models" chip is already there for the table only.

### 4. The "7 days" window is not 7 days after skip windows, so leaderboard numbers, "Change" and family/author weekly sums are inflated for days. Severity MEDIUM-HIGH, will recur on the next stall
- What: `derived()` takes the latest non-skip snapshot at or before D-7 (up to 10 days back), D-14, D-28, without rescaling. After a stall window the "7-day" figure covers up to 15 days; `dl_base7d = (all_7 - all_28)/3` assumes 21 days.
- Numbers (F_06, 389 days from 2025-04-05, today's skip list): window length != 7 on 63 days (16%); >= 10 days on 20 days (max 15, 2025-08-27 and 2026-09-10 = 14); 28-day reference longer than 28 on 72 days (up to 38, which biases growth by about -25% for days after a long skip, e.g. 2026-09-25 to 2026-10-01). Across top models, the median "growth_7d" went to +102% on 2026-09-08, +140% on 2026-09-10 (14-day window) and +237% on 2026-06-25; on 31 days more than half of the big models showed growth above +30%. The opposite direction too: median -33% on 2026-10-01.
- Ordering of "Most downloaded" changes little (the factor is uniform), but every number shown, the Change column, author `dl_7d` sums, `fam_7d` and the "growth_7d" thresholds are wrong on those days. Today (2026-10-04) windows are 7/7/28, so the current board is clean.
- Fix: interpolate dl_all at exactly D-7, D-14, D-28 (same linear spread the series use) or scale by 7/len. The same applies to `likes_7d`.

### 5. "Fastest growing" is dominated by one-day spikes and dormant models. Severity MEDIUM, a reviewer would likely raise it
- Current board: 37 of 100 entries have at least 50% of their 7 days in a single snapshot step, 5 of the top 25. #7 google-bert/bert-large-cased: 529,942 in 7 days of which 505,510 on one day (normal about 1.5k/day, +3,503%); #6 legraphista/Meta-Llama-3-70B-...-IMat-GGUF 93% in one day (42,958); #17 gbuzhf/KAT-Coder-V2.5-Dev-MTP-GGUF 95%; #22 lmstudio-community/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B-GGUF 95%; #54 utter-project/EuroLLM-1.7B-Instruct 99% (159,616 in one day). #1 HooshvareLab/bert-base-parsbert-uncased: +12,725%, from a 4-day burst of 16k-63k/day against about 100/day.
- Historical retro (F_08, 47 weekly boards since 2025-06-02, survivor-biased subset): on average 9 of the top 25 and 38 of the top 100 are single-step-dominated; the #1 was 80%+ one step in 8 of 47 weeks (not counting the 14-day-window days of finding 4).
- "New this month" (breakouts): 19 of 100 spike-dominated, 2 in the top 25; autotrust/GEV-26B-Decide 310,028 downloads in 7 days, 100% in one day (220,487) and `likes_7d` null; autotrust/JEV-27B-VL 725,796 in 4 days; autotrust/JEV-9B 91% one day; JoaoZaokk/LTX-2.5-22B-distilled-W4A8-ConvRot 98,676, 100% one day. "Most downloaded this week" is clean (1 of 100: CMSManhattan/JiRackUltra_1b, 890,673 in one day, rank 53, and it also tops the growth board on 2026-09-24).
- Suggested fix: require 3+ active days or cap a single day's contribution (for example at 3x the median of the other days) for growth and breakouts, or show "spike" in the UI. Naming: the internal key `gainers_7d` is a volume ranking (label "Most downloaded this week" is honest).

### 6. Renamed models have two histories; the old id is a dead 404 and the new one starts mid-way. Severity MEDIUM
- 256,812 ids exist in `series` but not in `models.parquet` (8,197 had >= 1,000 downloads at peak; 366 had >= 100k all time). Among them 993 clear rename pairs (old last_all within 3% of the new first_all, new first_day within 3 days of old last_day).
- Examples: cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 (48.10M all time, last seen 2025-03-06) -> cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L6-v2 (starts 2025-03-07 at 48.42M); THUDM/chatglm2-6b -> zai-org/chatglm2-6b (14,887,114 at 2025-07-27 and 2025-07-28); michellejieli/emotion_text_classifier -> michelleli99/ (2026-01-22, 16.24M); rednote-hilab/dots.ocr -> dots-studio/dots.ocr (2026-07-21); deepreinforce-ai/Ornith-1.0-9B-GGUF -> ornith-ai/ (2026-08-05, 5.34M).
- Consequences: the new page's chart and milestones start at the rename ("Before Mar 2025" for everything below 48M) and the old name 404s ("is not tracked"); `author_series` of the new owner gets a one-day step equal to the model's whole history: cross-encoder +145,617,119 on 2025-03-07 (501x its median daily step, visible in the author Weekly chart).
- Note: dl30 and dl_all counters carry over on rename, so no downloads are lost, only the page identity. Fix: map old ids to new ones through HF redirect (as `legacy_id` already does for org-less ids) and stitch the series, or mark rename days as skip for the author.

### 7. Frozen per-model counters: dl30 never decays. Severity LOW
- 1,316 models have identical dl30 and dl_all for 35+ days (impossible for a 30-day window); only 140k of 2.86B total dl30 (0.005%), 20 models above 1,000. Examples: alexrzem/flux-loras dl30 36,020, dl_all 36,638 unchanged since at least 2026-08-05, rank #4,413 on the Hub, last 7 days 0; TenjinDas/Test frozen at 8,327 since 2026-08-31; belztjti (10 models). 49 models with dl30 >= 1,000 show 0 for the last 7 days. A model page can say "last 30 days 36K, last 7 days 0".
- Fix: if dl30 and dl_all have not changed for >= 31 days, show dl30 as "no data" or set to 0.

### 8. Counters going down for individual models (lasting corrections). Severity LOW
- On the latest day 8 models with dl30 >= 1,000 have negative 30-day all-time delta, e.g. meshllm/gemma-4-E4B-it-Q4_K_M-layers dl_all 159,747 -> 51,555 in 30 days (dl30 13,146); meshllm/gemma-4-31B-it-qat-UD-Q4_K_XL-layers 29,636 -> 7,795; Vishva007/Qwen3.5-9B-W4A16-AutoRound-GPTQ 20,740 -> 2,504. The meshllm "-layers" repos are a family of artifacts (many, created 2026-09-12). The weekly chart clips negatives to 0, so the downloads before the drop are not lost, but later days are a hole. Not on any leaderboard.

### 9. Checks that came out fine
- dl30 vs dl_all(t) - dl_all(t-30) (F_02/F_03): consistent. On 3.9M model-days (dl30 >= 1000) the median ratio is 0.996-1.00 until mid-2025 and 1.02-1.03 afterwards (dl_all delta about 2-3% higher than dl30, consistent over time), 1% of model-days differ by more than 25%. Today, of 35,566 models with dl30 >= 1000, only 34 are under half and 119 above 150% (new models, meshllm layer repos, frozen counters, Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct +15%, google/timesfm-2.5-200m-pytorch 2.09x). The site shows the Hub's dl30 for "last 30 days" and the dl_all delta for "7 days", so the two cannot visibly contradict except for the frozen/negative cases above. `dl_7d` is capped at dl30 (verified: 796 models have dl_7d >= 90% of dl30, none above).
- Family totals: `family_series` last day vs `models.fam_dl30/fam_all`: 25,451 families, 7 differ by more than 0.1% (Wan-AI/Wan2.2-T2V-A14B 594,874 vs 597,060; BAAI/AREX-Base 5,087 vs 2,287 because of a duplicate id, see below). Authors: 50,603 compared, 4 dl30 mismatches (BAAI 149,115,088 vs 149,113,488; aidealab 20,448 vs 16,076, 11 vs 7 models), 6 member-count mismatches; these are models removed between the series snapshot and the meta join.
- Wrapped vs author_series over the 12 months: cross-encoder 651,056,662 vs 651,058,280; zai-org 118.7M vs 119.6M; amazon 304.32M vs 304.33M. Wrapped is immune to the 11-24 partial day and to the 2025-02-27 zero (per-model increments).
- Sparks: they use the same skip filter and gap spreading as the series; no new distortion found beyond finding 4's window logic. Low days are not skipped in any of them, which matches the pages' wording ("greyed out, not smoothed").

## Details and smaller items
- **Biggest families board shows the same family several times** (F_13): of the top 30, 6 entries descend from another top-30 entry (nreimers/MiniLM-L6-H384-uncased 241.57M "family" with its own dl30 16,113 and #2 sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 241.53M; microsoft/MiniLM-L12-H384-uncased 97.3M with cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L12-v2 93.7M and cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L6-v2 85.3M; Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B-Base and Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B; MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3 and Comfy-Org/MiniMax-H3; google/gemma-4-26B-A4B and -it). In the top 100, 30 entries descend from another entry. Severity LOW-MEDIUM; fix: keep only roots (no ancestor on the board), or label as nested.
- **Duplicates**: `models.parquet` has 8 duplicate ids (joins multiply rows; BAAI/AREX-Base appears 4 times in the family join and its family dl30 is 5,087 vs 2,287); series files have duplicate (id, day) keys (30 in 2026-10, 207 in 2026-09; not inspected further, so not confirmed that they double-count in sums). Severity LOW.
- Spark and "7 days" on the first day after a stall: episodes touching the last two days are left alone by `windows()`; `derived()` always keeps `last` even if frozen, so a board published on a frozen day understates by about a day for all models.
- Not checked: models vs `skip_days` effect for dataset boards (out of scope), the server-side heavy scans (none run).
