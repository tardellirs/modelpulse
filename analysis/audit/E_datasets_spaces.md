# Audit E: datasets and Spaces (read-only)

Data: HF dataset `modelpulse/model-pulse-data` as of 2026-10-05 (datasets series through 2026-10-04, 677 snapshot days; 121 calendar days have no snapshot). Raw snapshots on the server were read (read-only, docker `--memory 4g`, finished 10:15 UTC = 07:15 local) only for the coverage/seam/rename checks. Scripts are next to this file (`E_*.py`); small outputs `E_hub_daily_datasets.csv`, `E_coverage_sample.csv`, `E_space_boards_replay.csv`. Caveat: replays of past leaderboards use today's `skip_days`, which is not necessarily what the build knew on that day.

## Headline findings

### 1. The two big dataset "low runs" are an artifact: stall windows stop at the edge of a missing-snapshot block (severity HIGH, a reviewer will very likely raise it)
What: when a stall is followed by days with no snapshot, the catch-up lands in a gap-averaged block (every day of the block gets the same value). `stalls.windows` works on calendar days and treats the flat block as normal days (its own value dominates the 15-day median, so ratio = 1.0). The window takes only the first day of the block, so the stall total is spread over too few days (low run) and the rest of the catch-up stays flat and too high.
- 2025-06-25..06-30 (6 low days, all = 569,400): snapshots 06-25..06-29 are frozen (5 days, raw ~0), then one 10-day gap (06-29 to 07-09, 34.16M, 3,416,276/day). The window is 06-25..06-30: 6 x 569,400 = 3.416M, i.e. one day's worth of the block. Then 07-01..07-09 = 3,416,276 flat (9 days). True level over 06-24..07-09 is about 2.3M/day (34.2M + 1.9M over 15 days; neighbours are 2.0-2.7M). Chart shows a valley (0.57M) then a plateau (3.4M).
- 2026-07-10..07-14 (5 low days, deficit 15.0M, 25% of all dataset low-day deficit): raw 07-08 = 0 (stall), 07-09 = 17.19M, 07-10 = 4.58M, then 07-15 is a 5-day gap block (26.21M, 5.24M/day for 07-11..07-15), 07-16 = 14.07M, 07-17..07-23 a second window. The window for 07-15..07-16 does not include the gap days 07-11..07-14, which stay at 5.24M while 07-15/16 get 9.66M. Total 07-10..07-16 = 44.9M = 6.4M/day if spread.
- Same mechanism, smaller: 2026-01-28..30 (window 01-28..30 total 7.0M, then block 01-31/02-01 at 3.52M/day), 2026-03-17..18 (gap block 03-17/18 = half of a 2-day total, 2.83M/day, next day 12.3M), 2026-08-26 (gap block 08-26/27: window starts on 08-27 and cuts the 2-day block in half; 08-26 stays at 4.94M, then 08-28 = 0.01M, 08-29 = 26.03M).
Verified: raw pair totals per snapshot pair from the series (`E_02_raw_pairs.py`, `E_hub_daily_datasets.csv`, `E_05_lowdays.py`). 17 of the 35 low days are flat copies of a neighbour (spread windows / gap blocks) and 4 more sit in a gap block with no snapshot of their own; 12 of 21 low runs have more excess above the median in the next 7 days than their deficit (e.g. 2025-12-14: 1.3M deficit, 9.6M excess), so the sentence "low days that nothing makes up" in the README is wrong for datasets.
Fix: build windows over snapshot intervals, not calendar days: if a window touches a day of a gap block, extend it to the whole block (and compute the 15-day median on snapshot-interval totals so a flat block counts once). This should remove about half of the 35 low days (the runs above) and the 06-30/07-01 plateau.

### 2. Datasets low days: why 35 vs 24 (answer to question 1)
Not a concentration effect and not holidays. Breakdown of the 35 dataset low days (21 runs, deficit 63.5M = 2.4% of all downloads since 2025-03-05):
- ~19 days: finding 1 (gap blocks, windows cut or too short).
- 6 Sundays (2025-03-16, 05-18, 07-13, 12-14, 2026-06-14, 07-26): datasets have a weekly shape; Sunday mean ratio to the 15-day median is 0.92 (median 0.94), other days 0.99-1.08, and Sundays carry 8 of 35 low days with 0.7 as threshold. The 0.7 rule sits only 0.2-0.3 below a normal Sunday, with the very spiky dataset series (catch-up days 2-6x) it fires more often than for models. Fix: use a day-of-week-aware median (same weekday in the surrounding 3-4 weeks) or a lower threshold (0.6) for datasets.
- ~10 weekday days with genuinely low raw totals and no stall (2025-05-02, 05-10, 05-16, 2026-02-28, 04-01, 06-20, 06-24, 08-31, 09-25...). Several are the day before or after a 2-4x catch-up spike (2025-05-16/18 then 7.82M on 2025-05-19 = 4.3x median with 0.37% of datasets going down, unflagged; 2026-08-31 4.53M between 26.0M and 8.6M). These are partial freezes (Hub updating only part of the counters) that the "frozen >= 0.9" rule misses because dataset freezes are all-or-nothing in the rule but not always in practice. Concentration is not the cause: on normal days top-1 dataset = 4.1% of the daily total (median; max 58% once), top-10 = 19.6%, top-100 = 45.9%.
Severity MEDIUM. Fix: see findings 1 and 4 (flag high days too; weekday-aware median).

### 3. Unflagged HIGH days, including one single-repo event (severity MEDIUM; the reviewer has so far checked lows, highs are next)
Settled `hub_series` ratio > 1.4 of the median and not in a window: 2025-05-19 (7.82M, 4.3x), 2025-09-04 (6.40M, 2.6x: allenai/objaverse alone 3.74M = 58%), 2026-03-19 (12.30M, 2.2x), **2026-09-16 (21.64M, 2.7x, Wednesday, 66% of datasets moved, top-1 only 2.4%)**, 2025-12-21..23 (1.7-1.9x), 2026-06-12, 2026-08-29 (1.5x). Cause for 09-16 is not identifiable from the data (not a single repo; nothing frozen before it except the 09-09 stall whose catch-up on 09-10 was 12.79M). Fix: also list `high_days` (ratio > 1.8), and for single-repo events (top-1 share > 40%) say so on the chart.

### 4. "Fastest growing" and "Breakouts" datasets are dominated by one-day bursts (severity MEDIUM, reviewer likely)
Current leaderboards (2026-10-04): in `growth_7d` 8 of 100 repos have >= 70% of their 7-day sum in one day (5 have >= 90%); rank 1 Zellic/smart-contract-fiesta has 98,717 of 99,143 in one day (growth +87x); rank 5 AuthenticIlm/Shamela4_Full_DB 24,588 of 26,453 on the last day. `breakouts`: 16 of 100 with >= 70% in one day (13 >= 90%). `gainers_7d` top ranks are fine (5 of 100 >= 70%), e.g. transferable-samplers/many-peptides-md (979k, two days of ~570k/400k, rest ~0) is rank 2. Not a stall artifact, but looks like one to a reviewer. Fix: require activity on >= 3 of 7 days (or cap a day at 3x the median of the other days) for growth/breakouts; or show a "single-day" badge.

### 5. Leaderboard "7 days" windows are often not 7 days (severity MEDIUM for datasets, current day is clean)
Code: `build.derived` drops `skip_days` before `at_or_before(last-7, max_back=10)`, so when day-7 is a skip day the window silently becomes 8-17 days (the last day is always kept even if it is a stall: then 6/7 of a week). Replay on the day lists (`E_08_windows.py`): datasets, 478 build days since 2025-03-10: window != 7 days on 101 (21%): 8d on 37, 9-17d on 47, and **NULL on 17 days**. When the reference is missing, `dl_7d = min_horizontal(NULL, dl30)` = `dl30` (verified in polars: null is ignored), so the "7-day" boards show 30-day downloads. Days without a usable reference: 2025-07-12..15, 2025-11-24, 2026-04-19..22,24, 2026-06-11..17 (all based on today's skip list). 64 build days were themselves stall days. Every Wednesday stall also makes the following Wednesday an 8-day window (+14%). Spaces, same code path without skip days: window != 7 on 58 of 673 build days, and **likes_7d = all-time likes (fill_null(0)) on 2024-08-24/25, 2025-11-24, 2026-04-19..22,24, 2026-06-11..17**; replayed: on 2026-06-14 the "Most liked this week" #1 would be mteb/leaderboard with 7,474 "weekly" likes. Current state (2026-10-04) is clean. Fix: choose the reference snapshot on the non-skipped list but divide/scale by the real span (or refuse to publish a 7d board when the span != 7 +/- 1 days); for NULL use null, not dl30/all likes.

### 6. Spaces snapshot truncated to 1,000 rows on 2026-01-17..01-22 (severity LOW now, but visible in Space pages)
The source snapshots on those 6 days contain only 1,000 Spaces (76,824 vanish on 01-17, likes sum 345k vs ~946k, back on 01-23 with 73,579 "new" ids). Per-Space series have a 6-day hole (the front end averages across it, harmless), but `Total likes` for the Hub or any aggregate would show a cliff, and `first_seen`/"new Spaces" logic on 2026-01-23 sees 73k new Spaces. The dataset series have no such event (min `n_ids` is monotone; the 2026-06 drop is real, see 8). Fix: drop snapshot days whose row count falls below ~90% of the previous one (treat like skip_days) for Spaces too.

### 7. Spaces like purges (answer to question 4): real and large, handled only by clipping (severity LOW/MEDIUM)
Mass unlike days (number of Spaces losing likes / likes lost): 2025-04-11 411 Spaces / 18,869 (median -50 each, e.g. ginigen, openfree, fantos, VIDraft Spaces; Jesse311/FaceSeek 224 -> 0), 2025-01-17 118 / 6,799 (typ. -80 to -94), 2024-12-11 64 / 5,989 (up to -226), 2025-05-27 301 / 3,779, 2025-04-25 232 / 1,912, 2025-04-24 232 / 1,247. Normal day: median 14 Spaces / 16 likes. These are batch removals of farmed likes from the same group of orgs. 2025-09-12 (-2,527 total likes) and 2026-03-18 (-15,390) are Spaces deleted, not unlikes (n_gone 21 and 53). None of the 2026 purge days from the Daily Papers list (2026-09-16/18) shows in Spaces (largest 2026 unlike day: 2026-02-21, 51 Spaces / 583 likes).
Effect on boards: `likes_7d` uses `clip(0)` so purges never show as negative. Replay of 93 weekly "most liked this week" top-10s (`E_04_spaces_boards.py`): only 7 weeks have a top-10 Space that later lost >= 50% of its likes (11 slots), so the leaderboard is mostly not distorted. Space pages: weekly chart sums only positive daily changes (`daily()` clips), so a farmed Space shows its inflated gains while "Total likes" drops; 238 of 3,565 Spaces with peak >= 50 likes later lost >= 50% of the peak. Several current "most liked this week" entries look like the same pattern (steady 20-50 likes/day for weeks on anonymous "uncensored" Spaces: Pepe104/MiniMax-H3-Turbo-Lora-UNCENSORED 285, arudradey/qwen-image-2.1-uncensored-gguf 194), a reviewer could ask about it; I cannot prove farming. Suggest a footnote on the Space page and optionally a "likes were removed on <date>" marker.

### 8. Renamed and deleted datasets (severity LOW-MEDIUM)
- Renames split history: at least 500 pairs (query capped) where the old id ends and a same-name id under another org starts the next day with the same all-time counter (>= 1,000 downloads): LLM360/TxT360 -> IFM/TxT360 (3,323,179 both, 2026-07-28/29), haonan-li/cmmlu -> lmlmcat/cmmlu, CohereForAI/xP3x -> CohereLabs/xP3x (1,392,289), THUDM/LongBench -> zai-org/LongBench, lmms-lab/* -> lmms-lab-encoder/* (about 10 big ones on 2026-07-29/30), mcp-course/images -> context-course/images, uwipl/RT-Pose -> andaba/RT-Pose. The new page has history only from the rename day and the old page ends. Hub totals are unaffected (one day of the renamed repo is lost). `Repos.legacy_id` only resolves names without "/", so old org/name URLs should 404 (code reading; not tested live). Fix: map old -> new id (same dl_all within 2% on consecutive days) and redirect; optionally stitch history.
- Deleted/private: 1,237,633 ids in the series, 1,059,354 alive. Disappearing ids per month 4-10k, but **55,919 in 2026-06** (raw rows 1,035,941 on 06-19 -> 942,042 on 07-01); 100 of them had >= 10k all-time downloads. Deleted datasets stay in `hub_series` (flows keep their history, correct) but vanish from `datasets.parquet` and so from search/pages. Biggest gone: permutans/fineweb-bbc-news (5.44M), ksolovev/FineNews (4.96M, deleted 2026-09-25, was a 138k/day source in May 2026), daniilakk/nbchr_pdfs (2.74M). Case duplicates are negligible (1 pair).

### 9. Checks that came out fine
- Rollbacks: dataset counters going down for > 0.5% of ids only on 2025-05-19 (0.37%), 2025-12-19 (6.3%, 45k total), 2026-02-18/19 (0.9%/1.3%, 1.25M/1.08M downloads lost, a few repos) and tiny ones; none of them is a Hub-wide rollback like the model ones. Nothing is mis-counted as new downloads except the 2026-02-18/19 pair (total_neg 1.25M, partly offsetting positives of 4.88M/5.56M; not examined per repo).
- Coverage and seam (items 6, 7): flow of ids present in the series vs all raw ids, on 4 clean days (2025-04-09, 2025-05-29, 2026-06-19 .. 2026-09-30): 99.93-99.99%; stock 97.1% (2025-03) rising to 99.9% (2026-09) of all-time downloads of the series universe vs alive-today ids (`E_coverage_sample.csv`; "universe" there = ids alive today, so deleted datasets show as uncovered). The full build reads raw files (all ids), the daily job reads series files (universe only): the single daily-collected day is 2026-10-04; its n_new (2,067) and total (7.53M, ratio 1.00 to the median) show no step. Backfill vs daily seam: none detectable (datasets joined the site on 2026-10-04 with one daily day). Days 2026-10-01..03 have ~2% of flow from ids created that same week (not in the series yet), only a first-day effect.
- Backfilled series vs hub: all days from 2025-02-27 have `dl_all`; before that only dl30, so no daily downloads (documented).
- Per-id deltas in `hub_series` use only ids present on both days, so the first day of a new dataset never counts (small, consistent).
