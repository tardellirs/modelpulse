New report: what 19 months of daily downloads say about the Hugging Face Hub 📊

I went through the full download history in Model Pulse. A few findings:
- Qwen's own repos went from 9% to 30% of all text and vision-language model downloads (peak 50% in April); Meta's went from 10% to 3%
- 43% of all downloads go to derivatives (quantizations, fine-tunes, merges), up from 18%
- Unsloth grew 6x and LM Studio's community org 24x
- Rerankers grew almost 10x in a year, and BERT-style fill-mask models are the only big category that shrank
- Successful launches spike for two weeks, then plateau: 40% had their best week after month three

Full report, with charts, tables and methodology: https://huggingface.co/spaces/tardellirs/model-pulse?view=report
